## [Unreleased]

### Added

- This major includes every change of the third major up to v3.14.0; that history is in the third major's changelog, and the third-major fixes released after it are listed below as carried.
- bound: the `bound` package, `Duration` and `Count` with `Of`, `UnlimitedDuration` and `UnlimitedCount`, the three-state limit the limits and timeouts of the fourth major are written with, httpclient first: a field left out is absent and takes the framework's bounded default, a value below the field's minimum is refused naming the field, and unlimited is explicit, admitted only on the fields that say so; `Resolve` decides a field against its `Rule`, and the text and json forms read `unlimited`, a value or an absent key.
- cli: `output.NewUnboundedTablePrinter` answers a table printer with no width cap, the one `SelectPrinter` answers for an unlimited `TableMaxWidth`.
- bound: `Rule.DefaultUnlimited` makes an absent limit resolve as unlimited, for a field whose default is no bound, and such a rule admits an explicit unlimited too.
- exception: `NewHttpError(statusCode, HttpErrorConfig{Code, Key, Message, Parameters, Cause})` builds an http exception that declares a client-facing identity, a stable `code`, a translation `key` and the `parameters` it is rendered with, read back through `Code`, `Key` and `Parameters`; the kernel's error renderer serves them inside the envelope's `error` object beside the message, outside debug mode too, each left out when empty, the parameters copied in and out and nothing translated by the framework.
- container: `NewCloser[T]` builds the `Closer` a registration declares, the value form of the third major's `WithCloser` option.
- application: a `MiddlewareRegistration` lets an `HttpMiddlewareModule` register a middleware factory through the registrar, which the third major's two-method registrar could not.
- http: `ServerSentEventWriterConfig`, `ServerSentEventHubConfig` and `JsonHandlerConfig`, the configuration structs of the server-sent event writer, the hub and the json handler; `middleware.LimiterConfig`, `QuickLimitConfig` and `UserLimitConfig`, the configuration structs of the in-process limiters and the quick limit doors.
- cache: `InMemoryBackendConfig`, `ManagerConfig` and `RememberConfig`, the configuration structs of the in-memory backend, the manager and `Remember`; `cache/contract.TtlRule` and `ResolveTtl`, the one reading every backend gives the ttl of a write.
- session: `ManagerConfig`, `InMemoryStorageConfig` and `FileStorageConfig`, the configuration structs of the manager and the two storages.
- lock: `ExclusiveConfig`, `ExclusiveCommandConfig` and `LeaderGateConfig`, the configuration structs of `RunExclusive`, the exclusive command and the leader gate, the gate's `MaxCampaignBackoff` among them, 1m when left out; `lock/contract.LockTtlRule`, `ResolveLeaseTtl` and `ResolveLeaseRefreshTtl`, the one reading every locker gives the ttl.
- bound: `Resolved.Limit` answers a resolved limit as a limit again, so a value the configuration resolved is handed to a component in the state it was decided in.
- security: `ApiKeyHeaderAuthenticatorConfig`, `ApiKeyHeaderRuleConfig`, `HmacKeyRing`, `InMemoryTokenStoreConfig` and `MemoryNonceGuardConfig`, the configuration structs of the fourth major's security doors; `security/contract.TokenTtlRule`, `ResolveTokenTtl`, `NonceTtlRule` and `ResolveNonceTtl`, the readings of the token store and nonce guard ttls.
- security: `CompiledFirewallConfig`, `security/config.GlobalConfig`, `FirewallConfig` and `FirewallOverride`, the configuration structs of the compiled firewall and of the builder; `totp.Resolved`, the decided form of a `totp.Config`.
- container: `ContextOf`, the context the resolver a provider receives carries, the context of the call that started the creation, `context.Background()` for a resolver without one; `container/contract.ResolutionContext`, the optional capability behind it.
- container: `ContainerConfig`, the configuration struct of the container, and `CreationWaitersRule` with `DefaultCreationWaiters`; `ErrCreationWaitersExceeded`, the cause of a resolution refused because `ContainerConfig.CreationWaiters` resolutions already wait on the creation it would wait on, 1,024 when left out, at least 1, unlimited admitted.
- container: `ErrCircularDependency`, the cause of every refusal that would close a ring of dependencies.
- container: `ErrCloseDoorRequired`, the cause of a refusal for a service that carries `Close` without `CloseWithContext` and no registration `Closer` taking it.
- container: `ContainerConfig.TeardownSequential`, which closes the services one after the other instead of in dependency waves; `ApplicationConfig.ContainerTeardownSequential` and the key `MELODY_CONTAINER_TEARDOWN_SEQUENTIAL` (`kernel.container_teardown_sequential`) declare it for an application, the key winning, and `config/contract.KernelConfiguration` declares `ContainerTeardownSequential()`, answering the new `Switch`.
- container: `VerifyDeclaredTeardownDependencies() error` on the concrete container refuses a declared teardown dependency that names no container service; the application calls it at the end of its boot.
- container: `RegisterOptions.OptionalTeardownDependencyNames` and `OptionalTeardownDependencyTypes` declare a collaborator that may be absent: the edge orders the teardown when it is registered and is dropped without a refusal when it is not.
- application: `ApplicationConfig.ContainerCreationWaiters` and the key `MELODY_CONTAINER_CREATION_WAITERS` (`kernel.container_creation_waiters`) declare the container's creation waiters bound, the key winning over the field, a value below 1 failing the boot.
- bound: `Rule.Maximum` sets a ceiling on a limit, zero for none, and a value above it is refused as "the limit is above its maximum", naming the field, the value and the maximum.
- messagebus: `ConsumeCommandConfig` and `InMemoryTransportConfig`, the configuration structs of the consume command and the in-memory transport; `messagebuscontract.Envelope.WithStamps` adds several stamps in order.
- logging: `JsonLoggerConfig`, `DefaultLoggerConfig`, `ExitConfig` and `ShieldedStepConfig`, the configuration structs of the json and default loggers, the exit handler and the shielded step; `logging/contract.CauseChainDepthReporter`, the optional capability through which `LogError` reads how many links of an error's cause chain a logger writes, and `logging.CauseChainDepth`, the one door onto it, which a logger decorator forwards through.
- exception: `FromErrorAs` with `FromErrorConfig`, `FromError` at a level and with a context, the zero config answering what `FromError` answers.
- exception: `LogContextConfig`, the configuration of `LogContext`: `Extra` and `CauseChainDepth`, the links of the cause chain and of the cause context chain written, 8 when left out as the hard-coded depth was, at least 1, a negative one refused with a panic naming it.
- validation: `ValidatorConfig`; openapi: `DescribeConfig`, `ResponseDescription` and `Response[T]`; storage: `LocalStorageConfig`, the configuration structs of the validator, `DescribeTyped` and the local storage.
- container: `ForGoroutine(resolver)` and the optional capability `contract.GoroutineResolver` answer, for the resolver a provider receives, a resolver for a goroutine the provider starts: every resolution through it — a `Lazy` handle built over it or a plain `Get` — runs on a chain of its own even while the provider is still running, is recorded as the provider's dependency and carries no context, and while the provider's resolution is still building, a use that would wait on a creation that resolution holds is refused with `ErrCircularDependency`; the container, a scope and a resolver without the capability are answered as they are.
- container: the teardown walk reads an `atomic.Pointer[T]` a service holds through its own atomic load, so a collaborator held that way orders the holder before it as a plain pointer field does, at the same depth, where it was not seen; inside a value handed over by value the atomic word is not read.
- security: `NewSegmentPrefixMatcher(prefix)` and `SegmentPrefixMatcher`, a firewall matcher that claims the prefix and its tree under a `/` boundary, `/api` claiming `/api` and `/api/users` and never `/api-admin`, the reach of `accesscontrol.NewSegmentPrefixRule`: a trailing slash is folded, `/` claims every path, and an empty, relative or non-canonical prefix is refused naming the argument; the shadowed-firewall boot warning reads it at its segment boundary. The example declares its four tree firewalls with it.
- security: `securitycontract.RoleHierarchy` and the optional capability `securitycontract.RoleHierarchyBinder`, which `Compile` calls on a firewall's token source with its effective hierarchy.
- security: `MinimumSecretLength`, a `bound.Count` on `JwtConfig`, `HmacKeyRing`, `HmacTokenSourceConfig` and `HmacEnvelopeSignerConfig`, the fewest bytes a secret may hold: 32 when left out, the RFC 7518 floor of an HMAC-SHA256 key, at least 32, unlimited refused.

- security: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the access control section states that a rule's attributes are a conjunction, every one granted for the request to pass, and how to require either of two roles through the role hierarchy; that the matched rule a regex rule decided carries an empty `PathPrefix` and is named by its index and source; and the impersonation section that the switch role is read from the inner token's own roles unless the source is handed the role hierarchy through `ImpersonationTokenSourceConfig.RoleHierarchy`.
- http: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the middleware ordering section states that of two definitions sharing a name the later one's `allowDuplicates` and `replaceExisting` decide.
- translation: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the caveats state that a `#` inside a `plural` branch is always the number, with a parameter as the way to write a literal one; that a style that does not parse renders the empty string with no error; and that a locale is matched byte for byte, so the caller normalizes the request locale.
- config: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the `.env` section states that a `.env.<name>` file may overwrite the `MELODY_ENV` that selected it, and the secret parameters section that a parameter loaded from `.env` is not marked by its name, with the sweep an application following a naming convention can run before `Run`.
- application: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the lifecycle section states that `Run` is called once per process.
