# Changelog

All notable changes to `precision-soft/melody/v4` will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- This major includes every change of the third major up to v3.14.0; that history is in the third major's changelog, and the third-major fixes released after it are listed below as carried.
- bound: the `bound` package, `Duration` and `Count` with `Of`, `UnlimitedDuration` and `UnlimitedCount`, the three-state limit the limits and timeouts of the fourth major are written with, httpclient first: a field left out is absent and takes the framework's bounded default, a value below the field's minimum is refused naming the field, and unlimited is explicit, admitted only on the fields that say so; `Resolve` decides a field against its `Rule`, and the text and json forms read `unlimited`, a value or an absent key.
- exception: `NewHttpError(statusCode, code, key, message, parameters, cause)` builds an http exception that declares a client-facing identity, a stable `code`, a translation `key` and the `parameters` it is rendered with, read back through `Code`, `Key` and `Parameters`; the kernel's error renderer serves them inside the envelope's `error` object beside the message, outside debug mode too, each left out when empty, the parameters copied in and out and nothing translated by the framework.

### Changed

- config: **Breaking**: `config/contract.HttpConfiguration` declares `StaticExcludedPaths`, `SessionTtl`, `SessionTombstoneRetention` and `ShutdownTimeout`, which the third major carried on the sibling `ExtendedHttpConfiguration`; the sibling is removed, and a configuration of an application's own implements the four, where the third major read it with the defaults.
- session: **Breaking**: `RegenerateSession` joins `session/contract.Manager`, and the sibling `SessionRegenerator` is removed; `http.RegenerateRequestSession` calls it on the registered manager, where it refused a manager without the sibling.
- container: **Breaking**: `container/contract.ScopeManager`, and so `Container`, and `Scope` embed `ScopedRegistrar`, declaring `RegisterScoped` and `MustRegisterScoped`; `Application.RegisterScoped` registers on the kernel's service container directly, where it asserted the registrar and refused a container without it.
- http: **Breaking**: `SetMethodPolicy` joins `http/contract.Kernel`, and the optional `MethodPolicySetter` is removed.
- session: **Breaking**: `Snapshot` joins `session/contract.Session`, and the sibling `SnapshotSession` is removed; `session.Snapshot` calls it, where it read a session without the sibling through `All`, `IsModified` and `IsCleared`.
- event: **Breaking**: `NewEventDispatcherAdapter` takes the dispatcher alone, dropping the clock parameter the adapter never read.
- event: **Breaking**: `AddSubscriber` answers the `event/contract.SubscriberRegistration` that owns the installation and `RemoveSubscriber` takes it, so a value subscriber and a second installation of one subscriber install where the third major refused them, and the dispatchers keep no reference of their own to an installed subscriber; the sibling `SubscriberRegistrar`, with `AddSubscriberWithRegistration` and `RemoveSubscriberRegistration`, is removed.
- wiring: **Breaking**: `Scan` takes the build tags as a third parameter, nil for none, and `ScanWithBuildTags`, the name the third major gave that form, is removed.
- http: **Breaking**: `NewRequirements` takes `...*Requirement`, the pointers `NewRequirement` and the `Require*` helpers return, and refuses a nil requirement naming its index.
- httpclient: **Breaking**: `NewHttpClient` takes an `HttpClientConfig` struct and answers the client with an error, where the third major took the `*HttpClientConfig` that `NewHttpClientConfig(baseUrl, timeout, headers)` built and panicked on a refused base url or header map: the struct carries `BaseUrl`, `Timeout`, `Headers`, `Transport`, `RedirectsDisabled` and `MaxRedirects`, a field left out takes its default, and a base url path without its trailing slash, two spellings of one header, a limit below its minimum, an unlimited the field does not admit and `MaxRedirects` beside `RedirectsDisabled` are refused naming the field; `Timeout` is a `bound.Duration`, 30s by default, at least 1ms, unlimited admitted, where a non-positive timeout read as the default; `MaxRedirects`, a `bound.Count`, 10 by default, bounds the requests an exchange makes while it follows redirects, the first included as net/http counts its own cap, where the cap was fixed at ten.
- httpclient: **Breaking**: the `TransportConfig` fields are `bound.Duration` and `bound.Count`, where the third major read a non-positive value as not set: a field left out takes its default, a value below the field's minimum is refused, `IdleConnTimeout`, `MaxIdleConns` and `MaxIdleConnsPerHost` admit unlimited and the other timeouts refuse it, `ExpectContinueTimeout` admits zero, which sends the body at once, `KeepAliveDisabled` turns the keep-alive probes off and the new `IdleConnectionsDisabled` keeps no connection idle over HTTP/1 and HTTP/2, refusing an idle pool limit or idle timeout set beside it; `MaxIdleConns` and `MaxIdleConnsPerHost` are decided each on its own, 100 by default, where the third major copied a `MaxIdleConns` set to a positive value into the per-host pool.
- httpclient: **Breaking**: a request takes one `RequestOptions` struct, `Headers`, `Query`, `Body`, `Json`, `Timeout`, `BearerToken`, `BasicAuth` and `MaxResponseBodyBytes`, and the context first, where the third major took variadic `RequestOption` functions and ran every buffered request under `context.Background()`: `Get(ctx, url, options)`, `Delete`, `Request(ctx, method, url, options)`, and `Post`, `Put` and `Patch(ctx, url, body, options)` with the body still positional; cancelling the context ends the exchange and a nil context is refused; a `Body` and a `Json` together, either beside a positional body, and a bearer token beside a basic credential are refused, where the last option or the bearer won; `Timeout` left out is the client's and unlimited is no deadline; `MaxResponseBodyBytes` is 10 MiB by default, at least one byte, unlimited refused.
- httpclient: **Breaking**: `RequestStream` takes the context as its first parameter, the form the third major named `RequestStreamWithContext`; a `Timeout` left out or unlimited leaves the stream without a whole-stream deadline and a value bounds it, where a negative timeout bound the stream to the client's own timeout.
- cache: **Breaking**: `NewDefaultRememberOption`, and the zero-value `RememberOption` that reads as it, arms the cancelable flight, the last waiter leaving canceling the computation and the next caller leading a fresh one, so a hung callback no longer owns its key for the process's life; `WithCancelable(false)` is the detached flight the third major shipped as its default, and a wait timeout shorter than a callback that honours its context never stores under the default, so a bounded wait on a slow callback pairs with it.
- http: **Breaking**: the json body of every framework error, the renderer's and `JsonErrorResponse`'s, is the error envelope, `status`, `time` and the `requestId` where the request is known beside an `error` object carrying the `message`, the client-facing `code`, `key` and `parameters` an exception declares and, in debug mode, the `context` and the `cause`, where the third major kept the released `{error, time}` body with the debug detail at its top level; `validationErrors` stays at the top level, `ErrorResponsePayload` carries `Status`, `Time` and an `ErrorResponsePayloadDetail` under `Error`, and `NewErrorResponsePayload` takes the status before the message and the time.
- http: **Breaking**: `RequestAttributeSession` is `_session`, under the framework's underscore prefix with `_scheme`, where the third major kept its released value `session`; the kernel still publishes it after the route attributes.

### Removed

- httpclient: **Breaking**: `NewHttpClientConfig` with `WithoutRedirects`, `WithTransport`, `FollowsRedirects` and the getters, `DefaultTransportConfig`, `TransportDuration` and `TransportCount`, `HttpClient.SetBaseUrl` and `HttpClient.SetTimeout`, `RequestStreamWithContext`, the `RequestOption` function type with `NewRequestOptions` and the ten `With*` option functions, and the `AuthorizationOptions` and `BasicAuthorizationOptions` types and contracts are removed: a client is built from its configuration struct and stays as built, a caller that needs another base url or timeout building another client, and a request's options are one struct.
- messagebus: **Breaking**: the deprecated `HandleOptions.RequireHandler` is removed; refusing a message without a handler is the default, and `AllowMissingHandler` opts into the pass-through.
- debug: **Breaking**: `NewMiddlewareCommand` takes the description and the build providers, the form the third major named `NewMiddlewareCommandWithProviders`, and the one-provider form over `MiddlewareProvider` is removed.
- http: **Breaking**: `JsonHandlerFailureResponder` and `WithJsonHandlerFailureResponder` are removed, their form taking the names `JsonHandlerErrorResponder` and `WithJsonHandlerErrorResponder`, and the four-parameter responder with them.

### Fixed

- wiring: **Behavioural change**: carried from the third major (v3.14.1): `melody:wiring:generate` and `wiring.Generate` write the generated source in gofmt's form, indented with tabs, where it was indented with four spaces and every consumer's `gofmt -l` reported the file: the rendered source goes through `go/format` before it is written or printed, so the file written, the source on stdout and the source a staleness check regenerates in memory are the same bytes, and a source that does not format is refused with the rendered text in the error's context and no file written. A consumer regenerates its file once.
- application: **Behavioural change**: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): with an empty `MELODY_LOG_PATH` the container logger of a command writes to standard error, where it wrote to standard output in every process, so the boot records of a command that prints a document a caller redirects to a file (`melody:openapi:generate > openapi.json`) were written into the document; the http process still journals to standard output.
- mailer: **Behavioural change**: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): a custom header key is emitted trimmed, as the reserved and Message-ID checks already judged it, and a key that is empty or carries a byte outside the printable characters other than the colon is refused, where ` X-Campaign ` went out as a continuation of the previous header, an empty key as a line with no name, and `Message-ID ` suppressed the generated Message-ID while writing one no parser reads.
- http: **Behavioural change**: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the compression middleware sets the type it sniffs from the peeked bytes on a body that carries none before compressing it, as net/http does for an identity body and never for an encoded one, where a client that negotiated gzip received no `Content-Type` and one that did not received the sniffed one; a sniffed type the configuration excludes goes out uncompressed. Reached by a `FileResponse` of a file whose extension has no type.
- http: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the compression and cors middlewares hand a typed nil response on as nil, so the kernel answers it with its documented empty 204, where they dereferenced it and the request was answered 500 with a logged panic.
- messagebus: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): a consume worker reads the stop before it waits for a delivery, where a stop and a waiting delivery were both ready and Go picked between them at random, so a stopped worker took on average one more delivery per turn and spent the shutdown grace on work the operator asked to stop.
- security: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): `CompiledFirewall.Login` refuses a login handler's result that carries no token, as it refuses a nil result, where it dispatched `security.login.success` with the nil token and a listener reading it panicked.
- security: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): a path-prefix firewall whose prefix does not begin with a slash or carries an empty, `.` or `..` segment is named in the boot warning `security.firewallPrefixUnreachable`: the kernel refuses a request path that is not canonical before any firewall is selected, so such a firewall was silently never selected and its token source and access control never ran.
- application: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): `Boot` called from inside a module boot hook is refused, as every registration door is in the boot window, where it re-entered the phase loops, without end when the hook called it unconditionally.
- mailer: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): an attachment without a name, or whose name is empty once the characters a quoted name cannot carry are dropped, omits the `filename` parameter, where it wrote `filename=""`, which some clients render as a blank name.
- storage: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): `LocalStorage.Put` with a declared size stops reading one byte past it, where it read the whole reader, and wrote it to disk, before refusing the mismatch, so an endless reader filled the disk; a nil reader is refused by name before anything is created, where it panicked after creating the temp object; and a `Put` whose temp object was swept as stale while the write stalled says so, where it answered a bare "no such file".
- storage: **Behavioural change**: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): `LocalStorage` names an object only by a regular file: `Delete` of a key that resolves to a directory deletes nothing, where it removed an empty directory; `Exists` and `Delete` of a key that runs through a regular file answer `false` and `nil`, where they answered the operating system's "not a directory"; `Get` of a named pipe or a device is refused at once, where the open waited for a writer forever, and `Exists` answers `false` for it.
- mailer: **Behavioural change**: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): `Manager.Send` accepts in `Address.Email` only a bare addr-spec, which is what the renderer and the SMTP envelope write, where it accepted the parser's wider grammar — a display name, angle brackets, a comment, padding — and sent `RCPT TO:<Bob <bob@x.com>>`; a display name goes in `Address.Name`.
- config: **Behavioural change**: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): a `.env` line without a separator is refused on the last line of a file too, with or without a final newline, as on every other line, where it was read as the value of an empty key and dropped, so a `MELODY_ENV` written last without its value vanished and the process booted in the default environment.
- security: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the refusals of a JSON web token, of an internal-auth header and of a switch-user request carry the values the caller chose — the algorithm, the type and the key id read before any signature check, the claimed app, and the switch target — bounded to 512 bytes and naming the bytes dropped, as the kernel bounds the method and the path, where a 200 KiB header value reached the journal whole and an unauthenticated client could write about a megabyte into it per request.
- security: **Behavioural change**: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): `HmacTokenSource` reads an envelope's `IssuedAt` with its one-second resolution on the not-before check: the signer stamps its instant truncated down to the second, so a receiver whose clock trailed the sender's by a millisecond across a second boundary read the envelope as not yet valid and answered anonymous, about one envelope in five hundred at a 2ms skew with the default zero `Leeway`; the check now tolerates one second on top of the leeway, and the expiry and the nonce ttl are not widened by it.
- security: **Behavioural change**: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): `JwtTokenValidator` reads `nbf` and `iat` rounded down and tolerates their one-second resolution on top of `Leeway` when it checks that a token is not yet valid or issued in its future, where it read both rounded up and compared them with the clock as they were, so with the default zero `Leeway` a validator a millisecond behind the issuer across a second boundary refused a fresh token, and a fractional `nbf` or `iat` was refused during its whole first second with no skew at all, `iat` whenever `RejectFutureIssuedAt` is set, which `NewJwtTokenValidatorWithRevocationEpoch` forces; the expiry is not widened, and the revocation boundary reads `iat` rounded down as before.

### Documentation

- formatting: `README.md` and `.documentation/` read as one system, as the third major's do: every list bullet is `-`, the Go samples are indented with four spaces like the code, the shell samples are fenced `bash`, a forced line break is a trailing `\` instead of two invisible spaces, and no file keeps a double blank line or a blank line at its end.
- security: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the access control section states that a rule's attributes are a conjunction, every one granted for the request to pass, and how to require either of two roles through the role hierarchy; that the matched rule a regex rule decided carries an empty `PathPrefix` and is named by its index and source; and the impersonation section that the switch role is read from the inner token's own roles unless the source is handed the role hierarchy through `ImpersonationTokenSourceConfig.RoleHierarchy`.
- http: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the middleware ordering section states that of two definitions sharing a name the later one's `allowDuplicates` and `replaceExisting` decide.
- translation: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the caveats state that a `#` inside a `plural` branch is always the number, with a parameter as the way to write a literal one; that a style that does not parse renders the empty string with no error; and that a locale is matched byte for byte, so the caller normalizes the request locale.
- config: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the `.env` section states that a `.env.<name>` file may overwrite the `MELODY_ENV` that selected it, and the secret parameters section that a parameter loaded from `.env` is not marked by its name, with the sweep an application following a naming convention can run before `Run`.
- application: carried from the third major (v3.14.2): the lifecycle section states that `Run` is called once per process.
