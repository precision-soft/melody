# Changelog

All notable changes to `precision-soft/melody/integrations/bunorm/v4` will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- This major includes every change of the third major up to v3.6.0; that history is in the third major's changelog, and the third-major fixes released after it are listed below as carried.
- `diagnostics.go`: the logger the registry hands its providers forwards the wrapped logger's cause chain depth, as it forwards its threshold, so `logging.LogError` through it writes the chain the application's logger was configured for.
- `bound.go`: `Bound`, the manager registry bound to one runtime, whose `Manager`, `MustManager`, `Database`, `MustDatabase`, `DefaultManager`, `MustDefaultManager`, `DefaultDatabase`, `MustDefaultDatabase` and `MigrationDatabase` take no runtime, with `Runtime()` and `Registry()`, a zero view or one over no registry refused by its error doors with `manager registry is nil` and by its `Must…` doors with a panic carrying it; `For(runtimeInstance, registry)`, which allocates nothing, and `FromRuntime(runtimeInstance, serviceName)`, which resolves a registry the application registered under its own name, the module registering none.
- `split.go`: `SplitterConfig`, the one struct a splitter is built from, `ReplicaNames` and `ReplicaRetryInterval`, 5s when left out, at least 1ms, unlimited refused.
- `errors.go`: `ErrConnectionHostIsRequired` and `ErrConnectionDatabaseIsRequired`, the causes of the registry's refusal of an empty `Parameters.Host` or `Parameters.Database`.
- `audit/config.go`, `audit/recorder.go`, `audit/async_storage.go`: `RegistryConfig`, `RecorderConfig` and `AsyncStorageConfig`, the one struct each is built from.
- `encrypt/module.go`: the module is a `ServiceModule`; `RegisterServices` installs `ModuleConfig.Cipher` and each context's `Cipher` under its name for the columns of the process, in every process mode, and publishes the default cipher as `ServiceCipher`; a module with a `Cipher` and no database installs it and registers no command, and so does a context without a database.
- `encrypt/encrypt_database_command.go`, `encrypt/key_provider.go`: `EncryptDatabaseCommandConfig` and `KeyRing`, the one struct each door takes.
- `encrypt/errors.go`: `ErrSealFormatUnknown`, the cause of the refusal of a stored value that carries the encryption marker under a format this build does not read.
- `encrypt/cipher_registry.go`, `encrypt/module.go`, `encrypt/encrypted_string_for.go`, `encrypt/deterministic_for.go`, `encrypt/errors.go`: a seal bound to its column: `SealBindingReference`, a `CipherReference` whose `SealBinding()` names `<table>.<column>`, each part `[A-Za-z0-9_]{1,64}`; `EncryptedStringFor` and `EncryptedDeterministicStringFor` over such a marker seal under the format `gcm2`, the label, the key id and the format authenticated as associated data and taken into the deterministic nonce, so the seal opens through a column of that marker alone and equal values of two bound columns are not equal seals; `ModuleConfig.SealBindings` declares each one, a nil or pointer `Reference`, a marker with fields, with `SealBinding` on its pointer receiver or embedding `DefaultCipherReference` by pointer, a label outside the grammar, a cipher name the module installs no cipher under, a marker declared twice, a label declared twice over one cipher instance and a cipher implemented outside the package refused at registration naming the field and the index, an undeclared or misshapen bound marker refused at its column's first `Value` or `Scan`; `SealBindingConfig.RefuseUnbound`, false when left out, makes the column refuse a seal without a binding and a value without the marker before any key is asked for, `ErrUnboundValueRefused` in the chain; `DefaultCipherReference` selects the default cipher from a marker; a seal without a binding, `gcm1`, every seal of the third major included, opens as before.
- `encrypt/ciphertext_candidates.go`: `CiphertextCandidatesFor[R]`, the equality candidates of the deterministic column of a marker: for a bound one a `gcm2` candidate per active key, the current key first, followed by the `gcm1` ones while its binding admits unbound values.
- `encrypt/migrate.go`, `encrypt/encrypt_database_command.go`, `encrypt/module.go`: `TableSpecification.SealBindings` and `ColumnSealBinding`: over a bound column `mode=encrypt` seals a value without the marker under the binding and re-seals a seal without one under it, its key and mode kept, a bound seal left as it is; `mode=reencrypt` writes bound seals, a seal without a binding under the target key re-sealed with its mode kept; `mode=decrypt` opens under the binding and is refused before the first query over a binding that refuses unbound values; every mode stops at the row of a seal of another binding and, where the binding refuses them, of an unbound value, a column left unbound stopping at a bound seal with the message naming the binding as the repair, a spec it cannot bind refused before the first query; the width probe of a rotation counts both formats; `EncryptDatabaseCommandConfig.SealBindings`, the module handing each command the declarations of its compartment, binds a `--column` whose `<table>.<column>` is declared, `--seal-binding column=label` naming the label of a table spelled otherwise, a flag naming another label than the column's own declared one and a `--column` differing from a declared label only by case refused before any query, a label declared twice refused at construction, and the closing record names each bound column's label, `sealBindings`, and counts `boundFromUnbound` and `sealedFromPlaintext` per column value.

### Changed

- **Breaking** — `encrypt/module.go`, `encrypt/migrate.go`: `SealBindingConfig.RefuseUnbound` and `ColumnSealBinding.RefuseUnbound` are `UnboundAdmitted`, so a bound column refuses a `gcm1` seal and a value without the marker unless the door is set; a decrypt run over a column without the door is refused naming it.
- **Breaking** with the framework: the fourth major's runtime is read from `v4/container/contract` and built through `v4/container/runtime`, a runtime resolving through its scope only; `runtime.New` and `Runtime.Container()` are gone. See the framework's `UPGRADE.md`.
- **Behavioural change** — `encrypt/migrate.go`, `encrypt/schema_judge.go`: `MigrateEncrypt`, `MigrateReencrypt` and `MigrateDecrypt` run on one pinned connection and judge the specification against the schema before the first row: a column or a key matched letter case aside and used in the table's spelling, a name the table lacks or two names for one column refused, a paging key that is not the only column of a unique index or admits NULL under a unique index refused as `the paging key is not unique`, and a decrypt into a character column that is not `utf8mb4` refused under a `sql_mode` without `STRICT_TRANS_TABLES` or `STRICT_ALL_TABLES`, where such runs skipped rows, never ended over a spelling the charset lookup missed, or stored `?` over a plaintext.
- **Breaking** — `encrypt/encrypt_database_command.go`, `encrypt/module.go`, `encrypt/migrate.go`: `EncryptDatabaseCommandConfig.DatabaseResolver`, `ModuleConfig.DatabaseFactory` and `CommandContextConfig.DatabaseFactory` are `func(runtimeInstance containercontract.Runtime) (*bun.DB, error)`, handed the command run's own runtime, so the database the bulk command resolves at its first run is resolved through the run's scope under its context, where the factory took a context and the container the module captured; the module no longer needs a kernel with a service container for a factory and the two refusals of one are gone. `Migrator.MigrateEncrypt`, `MigrateReencrypt`, `MigrateDecrypt`, `EnsureColumnCapacity` and `EnsureColumnCapacityForReencrypt` take the runtime first, an absent one refused with `the runtime is nil or carries no context`. The audit recorder, tracker, storages and `BeginTransaction`, which run inside a bun transaction whose handle is the context, and `migrate.Up`, `Down` and `RunQueries`, called from a bun migration function, stay on a context.
- **Breaking** — `audit/config.go`: `Registry.EnsureSchema(runtimeInstance, database)` takes the runtime first, its statements run under the runtime's context and an absent runtime is refused with `the runtime is nil or carries no context` before any statement; a schema door is called from a service built at boot and resolved by what writes the trail, so a schema that cannot be created is a boot warning and the first audited write ensures it again under its own runtime, or from a command under its run.
- **Breaking** — `manager_registry.go`: `NewManagerRegistry(definitions, ManagerRegistryConfig{Logger})` takes the definitions as a `[]ProviderDefinition` and an optional logger in its config, and captures no context; an absent logger is the emergency logger, a typed nil refused as `manager registry logger is a typed nil` naming `ManagerRegistryConfig.Logger`, and `ErrLoggerIsRequired` is removed; `NewManagerRegistryWithContext` is removed.
- **Breaking** — `provider.go`: `Provider` has one open door per purpose, `Open(runtimeInstance, parameters, logger)` and `OpenForMigration(runtimeInstance, parameters, logger)`, each taking the runtime it runs under first; `ContextOpener`, `MigrationContextOpener` and the optional `MigrationProvider` are removed, so a provider with nothing to tune for migrations answers its `Open` from `OpenForMigration`, and the registry probes no capability.
- **Breaking** — `manager_registry.go`: `Manager`, `MustManager`, `Database`, `MustDatabase`, `DefaultManager`, `MustDefaultManager`, `DefaultDatabase`, `MustDefaultDatabase` and `MigrationDatabase` take the runtime first, and the provider is handed it over a context the caller's own context and the registry's `CloseWithContext` both end; a nil runtime, a typed nil and one carrying no context are refused with "the runtime is nil or carries no context", one carrying no scope with "the runtime carries no scope", before the lock is taken, and a caller whose context is already done is refused before an open starts, nothing dialled, held or memoized, `MigrationDatabase` refusing it ahead of a connection already kept for the name.
- **Breaking** — `manager_registry.go`: `MigrationDatabase(runtimeInstance, name)` answers `(*bun.DB, error)`, the `dedicated` bool removed: every answer is the provider's `OpenForMigration`, kept as the name's migration connection and released by `CloseMigrationDatabase`, except an answer that is the very pool the registry holds for the name, which is shared with it by identity, takes no hold and is never closed by a release, whichever of `MigrationDatabase` and `Manager` opened it first.
- **Breaking** — `manager_registry.go`: `SetLogger` is removed; the logger is the constructor's for the registry's whole life, so a registry is built where the application's logger resolves, inside its provider.
- **Breaking** — `split.go`: `ReadWriteSplitter.Writer(runtimeInstance)` and `Reader(runtimeInstance)` take the runtime first; a replica open the caller's own context ended is answered as that refusal, the replica not remembered unreachable and the primary not opened in its place, and the lone probe of a remembered replica gives its reservation back, so the next reader probes it.
- **Behavioural change** — `manager_registry.go`: concurrent callers of one name coalesce onto the first caller's open; a waiter whose own context ends leaves with its cause, the open going on for its opener, and a waiter whose context is live opens again when the open it waited on ended with its opener's context done, whatever the failure carries, a driver failure or a custom cause, while a waiter whose own context is done then answers its own refusal, so no caller receives another's cancellation.
- **Breaking** — `manager_registry.go`: the registry refuses a definition whose `Parameters.Host` or `Parameters.Database` is empty, by its position and name, naming the field `ProviderDefinition.Parameters.Host` or `ProviderDefinition.Parameters.Database`, where an empty host was refused at the first open and an empty database dialled on mysql.
- **Breaking** — `connection_parameters.go`: the deprecated alias `ConnectionParams` is removed; `ConnectionParameters` is the one shape.
- **Breaking** — `manager.go`, `manager_registry.go`, `audit/recorder.go`, `audit/async_storage.go`: `Manager.CloseWithContext` replaces `Manager.Close`: the database's close runs on a goroutine of its own, every call waiting on that one close, and the wait ends at the deadline with its error while the close goes on in the background, where it waited for the connections still checked out without a bound, and a panicking driver close is answered as the close's error; the registry hands each manager's close and each held migration database's close the teardown's deadline, where a migration database closed without a bound. `ManagerRegistry`, `audit.Recorder` and `audit.AsyncStorage` lose `Close`, `CloseWithContext` their one door, and a recorder owning its storage closes it through the storage's `CloseWithContext` alone; `NewRecorder` refuses `OwnsStorage` over a storage carrying `Close` without `CloseWithContext`, naming `RecorderConfig.OwnsStorage`, where its close was called.
- **Breaking** — `split.go`: `NewReadWriteSplitter` takes the registry, the primary's name and a `SplitterConfig`, a `ReplicaRetryInterval` outside its rule panicking naming the field where a zero interval took the default; `NewReadWriteSplitterWithOptions`, `ReadWriteSplitterOption` and `WithReplicaRetryInterval` are removed.
- **Breaking** — `audit/config.go`: `NewRegistry` takes a `RegistryConfig{DefaultTable, GlobalIgnoredFields}`, a table that is not a plain SQL identifier refused naming `RegistryConfig.DefaultTable`.
- **Breaking** — `audit/recorder.go`: `NewRecorder` takes the storage and a `RecorderConfig{Registry, OwnsStorage, Logger}`, an absent logger the emergency logger, the one an `AsyncStorage` left without one dead-letters through, a typed nil refused as `audit recorder logger is a typed nil` naming the field, the logger set once; the `(database, table)` door, `NewRecorderWithStorage`, `NewRecorderOwningStorage` and `Recorder.WithLogger` are removed.
- **Breaking** — `audit/async_storage.go`: `NewAsyncStorage` takes the delegate and an `AsyncStorageConfig{BufferSize, Logger}`, `BufferSize` 1024 when left out, at least 1, unlimited refused, where a non-positive size took 1024, the logger set once, the emergency logger when absent, a typed nil refused as `async audit storage logger is a typed nil`, a field outside its rule panicking naming it; `AsyncStorage.WithLogger` is removed.
- **Breaking** — `audit/storage.go`: the `Storage` contract's `Save` takes the entries as a `[]Entry`, where it was variadic; a nil or empty slice is a no-op on `BunStorage`, `FileStorage` and `AsyncStorage`.
- **Breaking** — `encrypt/cipher_registry.go`: `UseCipher` and `UseCipherNamed` are removed; the encrypt module installs the ciphers and the seal bindings when it registers its services, replacing the whole cipher and binding tables an earlier registration installed, which keeps none of its compartment names or bound markers, and the refusal of a column with no cipher names the module.
- **Breaking** — `encrypt/module.go`: a module with no `Cipher` and no `Contexts` is refused at registration, where it registered nothing; a repeated context name is refused, and so is a prebuilt database not of the MySQL dialect, before any cipher is installed; a second encrypt module in one application is refused by the framework, which reads a module's name as its identity, so every compartment and every binding is declared on one `ModuleConfig`; every refusal names the field and, on a context, its index.
- **Breaking** — `encrypt/encrypt_database_command.go`: `NewEncryptDatabaseCommand` takes the cipher and an `EncryptDatabaseCommandConfig{Database, DatabaseResolver, CommandName}`, exactly one of the two database fields, both or neither refused naming them, a typed-nil cipher refused; `NewEncryptDatabaseCommandWithName`, `NewEncryptDatabaseCommandFromResolver` and `NewEncryptDatabaseCommandFromResolverWithName` are removed.
- **Breaking** — `encrypt/command.go`: `Commands` takes the cipher and an `EncryptDatabaseCommandConfig`, as `NewEncryptDatabaseCommand` does; `CommandsFromResolver` is removed.
- **Breaking** — `encrypt/encrypted_string.go`, `deterministic.go`, `encrypted_string_for.go`, `deterministic_for.go`, `key_provider.go`: the deprecated `GoString` of the four column types and of `StaticKeyProvider` is removed; `Format` answers `%#v` and keeps the redaction.
- **Breaking** — `encrypt/key_provider.go`: `NewStaticKeyProvider` takes a `KeyRing{CurrentKeyId, Keys}`, its five refusals naming `KeyRing.CurrentKeyId` or `KeyRing.Keys`.
- **Breaking** — `encrypt/migrate.go`: `TableSpecification.BatchSize` is a `bound.Count`, 500 when left out, at least 1, unlimited refused, a run outside the rule refused before its first query naming the field, where a zero or negative size took 500; `--batch -1` is refused naming the flag and the field.
- **Breaking** — `encrypt/cipher.go`, `encrypt/migrate.go`: a stored value beginning with the encryption marker and its glue under a format this build does not read is an error, `ErrSealFormatUnknown` in its chain and the format named when it is one a build could have written, on the package's `Cipher.Decrypt`, on the `Scan` of the four column types over it and in every mode of the `Migrator` whatever its cipher, which stops at the row with no update, its width check leaving such a value out, where it was read as plaintext, passed through, and sealed again as data by the next write or the next `encrypt` run; the checks that a decrypted or written value is itself marker-shaped read the same family; through the package cipher a value an application writes in that shape is still sealed as data and reads back, and through a cipher implemented outside the package a column's `Value` and the `Migrator`'s re-seal refuse it as they refuse a `gcm1` one.
- **Breaking** — `encrypt/cipher.go`: `Cipher.Decrypt` and the `Scan` of `EncryptedString` and `EncryptedDeterministicString` refuse a seal bound to a column, `ErrSealBound` in the chain, as does a `…For` column whose marker has no binding; a `Migrator` run over a column its spec does not bind stops at the row of such a seal.
- `audit/async_storage.go` and `audit/recorder.go`: the log records are built through `exception.LogContext(err, exception.LogContextConfig{...})`, the fourth major's form, which takes the extra context in a struct; nothing of this module's own surface changes.
- **Breaking** — `audit/storage.go`: a `FileStorage` line names every key in camelCase, `{"table":…,"entry":{"id":…,"transactionId":…,"entity":…,"entityId":…,"operation":…,"changes":…,"actor":…,"createdAt":…}}`, where the entry's keys were the Go field names, and `changes` is the json array the entry holds, where it was that array escaped into a string; a `Changes` that is not a json array is written as a json string. `audit.Entry` carries no json tags, so an application marshalling it in its own documents is unaffected. ### Removed

- **Breaking** — `audit/async_storage.go`: `AsyncStorageConfig.CloseGrace` and its rule; `CloseWithContext` splits a deadline its caller declares into its two stretches without a cap, and with no deadline it drains the queue and joins the worker however long it takes, a cancellation still ending it, the application's `MELODY_TEARDOWN_TIMEOUT` the one source of the bound.

### Fixed

- `encrypt/encrypt_database_command.go`, `encrypt/migrate.go`: the command's closing record keys a bound column in the spelling the run resolved against the schema, where it carried the `--column` spelling; two `SealBindings` keys naming one column, letter case aside, are refused.
- `encrypt/migrate.go`: a write the server refuses is reported with its number and SQLSTATE in the message, the table, the columns and the row's key in the context and a `*mysql.MySQLError` of that number in the chain, the server's message, which can quote the plaintext a decrypt writes or an encrypt guards, withheld from the journal and from stderr; `EnsureColumnCapacity` and `EnsureColumnCapacityForReencrypt` read the columns in the table's spelling.
- `audit/change.go`: `ChangeSet` leaves out a field whose `bun` tag carries `scanonly`, bare, after a column name or beside another option, and a relation named before its `rel` or `m2m` option, a comma inside a parenthesised or quoted option value not splitting the tag, where the before image never loaded a scan-only field and every update recorded it as changed.
- `manager_registry.go`: the database that loses the migration open race and a database opened into a registry closed meanwhile are closed after the registry lock is released, before the open's waiters are, where a close the driver parked held every other registry call.
- `audit/track.go`: `Tracker.Update`, and `Tracker.Delete` under `CaptureDeleteBeforeImage`, read the stored row into a model holding the primary key alone, where they read it into a copy of the new model and a column that decodes into what is already there, a json map, kept the new model's keys: an update that only added a key was recorded with an empty change set.
- `split.go`: once `ReplicaRetryInterval` lapses one reader dials the unreachable replica and the readers beside it keep the primary, where every reader of that replica waited on the one pending open for the provider's whole retry budget.
- `audit/recorder.go`, `audit/async_storage.go`: the dead-letter record carries the entry's `actor`, `transactionId` and `createdAt`, where a replayed entry could not say who made the change.
- `encrypt/encrypt_database_command.go`: `--target-key` outside `--mode=reencrypt`, `--deterministic` with `--mode=decrypt` and one `--seal-binding` label named for two columns are refused naming the flag, where the first two were ignored and the run exited 0.
- `audit/async_storage.go` — carried from the third major (v3.6.1 and v3.6.2): `AsyncStorage.CloseWithContext` reads a deadline reached during one of its two stretches as the budget running out, never as its caller cancelling the close — abandoned while it waited for a save that ignored its cancellation, the budget spent during the drain or with the drain's timer — and every answer the deadline ends carries `context.DeadlineExceeded`; when the deadline, not the second grace, ended the wait, the answer says the deadline cut the grace short and carries `waited` beside the planned `grace`.
- `audit/async_storage.go` — carried from the third major (v3.6.2): `AsyncStorage.CloseWithContext` reached with a context that had already ended carries that context's error as the cause of its answer: a deadline passed before the close began is read off the answer as `context.DeadlineExceeded`, as a deadline reached during a stretch is, where the answer carried no cause; and a context its caller cancelled before the close began is answered as the caller's withdrawal with `context.Canceled` as its cause, where it read that the budget was already spent.
- `audit/async_storage.go` — carried from the third major (v3.6.3): the close that abandons a save which ignored its cancellation carries the deadline as its cause and says the deadline cut the second drain grace short also when the stretch's timer and the deadline were ready in the same instant and the wait answered on the timer, where that pick of the race answered with no cause and read "after a second drain grace"; and a close its caller cancels once it has begun, with no grace left to observe a reaction in, is answered as the caller's withdrawal after the close began, where it read that the close was cancelled before it began, the context being read at entry once, before anything waits.
- bunorm: an error whose `Unwrap` chain reaches itself no longer holds the goroutine that reads it: a provider's in the registry's open and the read-write splitter, a storage's or a unit of work's in the audit tracker, and a run's in the encryption migrator.

### Security
