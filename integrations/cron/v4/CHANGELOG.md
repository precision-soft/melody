# Changelog

All notable changes to `precision-soft/melody/integrations/cron/v4` will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `generate_command.go`, `module.go`: `ModuleConfig.Generator`, a `GeneratorConfig`: `DirectoryMode`, the permission of every directory the generator creates, the destination's parents, the logs directory and a log file's subdirectory, `DefaultGeneratorDirectoryMode` `0755` when left out as before, permission bits only with the owner's read, write and search, refused at registration naming `ModuleConfig.Generator.DirectoryMode`; `LogsDirectoryCheckDisabled` skips the judgement of the logs directory a root-run generator makes.
- **Breaking** — `runner_command.go`, `runner_config.go`: the in-process runner refuses at construction an entry naming `EntryConfig.User`, `ErrUnsupportedRunnerEntry` naming the command, the user and the door, where it ran it as the process user with one warning at `Run`, now gone; `RunnerConfig.EntryUserIgnored` admits such an entry.
- `tick_dedup.go`, `runner_config.go`, `configuration.go`, `runner_command.go`, `errors.go`: the in-process runner deduplicates ticks across replicas: before it runs a due entry it acquires a lock named after the entry and the minute, `<namespace>:cron:tick:<command>:<digest>:<unix minute>`, `melody:cron:tick:` under the default namespace, and keeps it past the command's end, for `RunnerConfig.TickDedup.Window`, five minutes when left out, and for the window plus `ClockJumpResetThreshold` when the entry pins a minute and an hour, so a replica that evaluates the minute later skips it. On by default over `RunnerConfig.TickDedup.Locker`, the application's `service.lock.locker` when left out, looked up once per `Run` in the run's scope; `TickDedup.Disabled` turns it off for the runner and `EntryConfig.TickDedup.Disabled` for one entry, and an entry names its own `Window` and `FailureMode`. A locker that cannot be reached leaves the tick not run and reported as that run's failure under `ErrTickDedupUnavailable`; `TickDedupFailureRun` runs it instead. A runner with no locker deduplicates nothing and says so in one warning at start. A window below 1s or unlimited, a call timeout below 1ms or unlimited, an unknown failure mode (`ErrUnknownTickDedupFailureMode`), a typed-nil locker and a key prefix holding whitespace or a control character are refused at `NewRunnerCommand` naming the field. `TickSkipReasonHeldByAnotherInstance` is the reason a skipped row carries.
- `parameter.go`, `module.go`: the module declares its ten `.env` keys through the framework's `EnvironmentKeyModule`, `MELODY_CRON_USER`, `MELODY_CRON_LOGS_DIRECTORY`, `MELODY_CRON_BINARY`, `MELODY_CRON_DESTINATION_FILE`, `MELODY_CRON_HEARTBEAT_PATH`, `MELODY_CRON_HEARTBEAT_ENABLED`, `MELODY_CRON_TEMPLATE`, `MELODY_CRON_K8S_IMAGE`, `MELODY_CRON_K8S_NAMESPACE` and `MELODY_CRON_K8S_RESTART_POLICY`, each feeding its `melody.cron.*` parameter and taking `ModuleConfig.KeyDefaults` or the module's default when no `.env` file sets it; the heartbeat switch, the RFC 1123 namespace and the `OnFailure` or `Never` restart policy are judged at boot in every mode the module is registered in, refused naming the key and the `cron.KeyDefaults` field, where they were judged only when `melody:cron:generate` ran; `KeyDefaults` sets the defaults.
- This major includes every change of the third major up to v3.8.0; that history is in the third major's changelog.
- `runner_config.go`: `RunnerConfig.OutputCaptureLimit` bounds what one run writes into the journal, 64 KiB when left out as the constant was, at least 1, unlimited refused; `RunnerConfig.ClockJumpResetThreshold` is the wall-clock jump that re-anchors the minute chain, three hours when left out as the constant was, at least two minutes, in whole minutes, unlimited refused; `RunnerConfig.Timeout` and `RunnerConfig.ShutdownTimeout` are what an entry that names no timeout or window of its own runs under.

### Changed

- `tick_dedup.go`: the dedup's default key prefix reads the namespace through the framework's `config.NamespaceFromRuntime`, `melody` when the run carries no configuration, as before.
- **Behavioural change** — `runner_command.go`: an application that registers `service.lock.locker` gets the tick dedup with no change of its own: on several replicas a tick that ran once per replica runs once, the others reporting it under `skipped`, a list every minute's document now carries, empty when nothing was skipped, and the text line counts; a session locker, the mysql and pgsql ones, holds one connection per held tick; a locker outage fails the due runs instead of running them. See `UPGRADE.md`.
- `runner_config.go`, `runner_command.go`: the GoDoc no longer presents `lock.NewExclusiveCommand` as the runner's protection on several replicas; it serialises overlapping runs of one command, and the tick dedup is the runner's.
- **Breaking** — `template.go`, `template_crontab.go`, `template_k8s.go`, `generate_command.go`, `entry.go`: `Template` carries `OwnershipMarker(applicationName string) string` and `RendersUserColumn() bool`, `OwnedTemplate`, `UserColumnTemplate` and the name fallback removed; `RenderOptions.ApplicationName` hands every template the cli name, so a template embedding a builtin renders the application's line and its `--prune` sweeps; a name carrying a control character is refused with `ErrInvalidApplicationName` by the generator, where only a line break was, and by the builtins' `Render` and `cron.Render`, `ValidateApplicationName` exported for a custom dialect. See `UPGRADE.md`.
- **Breaking** — `module.go`, `generate_command.go`, `parameter.go`: `ModuleConfig.Configuration` is the factory `func(kernelInstance kernelcontract.Kernel) *Configuration`, `ConfigurationFactory` removed, a nil answer refused naming the field; `ModuleConfig.Templates` installs templates after the builtins, a nil entry refused naming its index; `NewGenerateCommand` and `EnvironmentKeys` are unexported, and `GenerateCommand.RegisterTemplate` and `Commands(configuration)` are removed, the module the one door that builds the generator and declares its keys. See `UPGRADE.md`.
- **Breaking** — `runner_config.go`, `configuration.go`, `runner_command.go`: `RunnerConfig.GracefulTimeout` and `EntryConfig.GracefulTimeout` are `ShutdownTimeout`, the one name of the stop grace across http, consume and cron, their refusals naming `RunnerConfig.ShutdownTimeout` and `EntryConfig.ShutdownTimeout` the log key `gracefulTimeout` and the abandon errors' context key `unwindGrace` reading `shutdownTimeout`; the entry's field still bounds the unwind after its own `Timeout`. See `UPGRADE.md`.
- `runner_command.go`: **Breaking** with the framework: the runner reads the fourth major's runtime from `v4/container/contract` and opens each run's child scope through `runtime.NewChildRun`; a runtime whose scope cannot open one fails that run naming the command, where a runtime without a container was a panic the dispatch contained.
- **Breaking** — `parameter.go`, `module.go`: a parameter the application still registers as `melody.cron.logs_dir`, the third major's name, refuses the boot when the module registers its commands, in every process shape, as `the parameter melody.cron.logs_dir is retired: rename it to melody.cron.logs_directory`, without the value, where it was read by nothing and the generator redirected under the default directory. The check runs after the resolve, no earlier hook reading the parameters: a value still naming `%kernel.logs_dir%`, the third major's default, is refused first by the resolve as an undefined parameter, and the retirement on the next boot.
- `parameter.go`: a `.env` line setting `MELODY_CRON_LOGS_DIR`, a spelling of the unreleased fourth major, refuses the boot through `EnvironmentKey.Retired` on the module's key, an empty value and `MELODY_CRON_LOGS_DIRECTORY` beside it included, in the framework's one refusal `the environment key MELODY_CRON_LOGS_DIR is retired: rename it to MELODY_CRON_LOGS_DIRECTORY`, without the value. The module needs the core release that carries `EnvironmentKey.Retired`, the first fourth-major release.
- **Behavioural change** — `generate_command.go`, `errors.go`: the logs directory is created once the entries are known, and with the `crontab` template a generator running as root refuses, before it creates or writes anything, a missing logs directory a user writes its log file or the heartbeat directly in, or an existing one such a user cannot write and enter, or a subdirectory writer cannot enter, with `ErrLogsDirectoryNotWritable`, naming the directory and the users, sorted, where it created the directory root's and the entry's shell could not create its log file; a user the host does not know, uid 0, unreadable groups, a directory with an access control list and an existing writable log file are not judged.
- `runner_command.go`: a scheduled command's document carries the application's version: the runner reads it off the tree it was started under and hands it to each dispatch, which runs on the process's context under no tree of its own and read it empty.
- `module.go`, `parameter.go`: **Behavioural change**: the module's defaults are no longer opt-in: with no `--out` the generator writes `%kernel.project_directory%/generated_conf/cron/crontab` and with no `--logs-directory` it redirects every entry under `%kernel.logs_directory%/cron`, creating that directory on every run that is not refused, the k8s template's included, where a module registered without `WithDefaultParameters` refused both runs naming the flag; an empty `.env` line for `MELODY_CRON_DESTINATION_FILE` or `MELODY_CRON_LOGS_DIRECTORY` restores the refusal.
- `generate_command.go`: **Behavioural change**: the heartbeat opt-in reads an empty value as unset, the declared key's default, where an empty parameter was refused as not holding a boolean.
- `runner_command.go`: **Behavioural change**: the child scope of a run closes under the container's `ScopeTeardownTimeout`, 3s when left out, where a scoped service waiting on its context held the runner without limit; a service that did not finish is reported as the run's close failure.
- `runner_command.go`: the log records are built through `exception.LogContext(err, exception.LogContextConfig{...})`, the fourth major's form, which takes the extra context in a struct; nothing of this module's own surface changes.
- **Breaking** — `runner_command.go`, `runner_config.go`, `configuration.go`, `module.go`: `NewRunnerCommand` takes the configuration and one `RunnerConfig` of `Dialect`, `Commands`, `OutputCaptureLimit`, `ClockJumpResetThreshold`, `Timeout` and `ShutdownTimeout`, resolved once, a value outside its rule panicking naming the field, where it took the dialect and a variadic command list; `ModuleConfig.Runner` replaces `RunnerCommands` and `RunnerDialect`. `Configuration.Schedule` takes the `EntryConfig` by value and `ScheduledCommand.Config` is a value; `EntryConfig.Instances` is a `bound.Count`, 1 when left out, at least 1, unlimited refused, a negative one refused naming the field by the generator and by `NewRunnerCommand` where it was read as 1; `EntryConfig.Timeout` and `EntryConfig.ShutdownTimeout` are `bound.Duration`, the runner's when left out, at least 1ms, an unlimited timeout admitted, an unlimited window refused, an entry refusal naming the field and the command, where a negative timeout meant no deadline and a non-positive window the default.
- **Behavioural change** — `runner_config.go`: an entry that names no timeout is cancelled after one hour, `RunnerConfig.Timeout`'s default, where it ran with no deadline; an entry that wants none writes `Timeout: bound.UnlimitedDuration()`, and a runner that wants none for every such entry writes it on `RunnerConfig.Timeout`.
- **Breaking** — `entry.go`, `template_k8s.go`: `Entry.Args` is renamed `Entry.Arguments`, and the k8s template's refusal of an empty token names the field `Arguments`; nothing else changes.
- **Breaking** — `parameter.go`, `generate_command.go`, `errors.go`: the logs directory is spelled out: the flag `--logs-directory`, the parameter `melody.cron.logs_directory`, `ParameterLogsDirectory` and `ErrNoLogsDirectory`, where they said `logs-dir`, `logs_dir` and `LogsDir`; the refusals say "logs directory" and a log file name escaping it is reported under the context key `logsDirectory`. The old flag is refused by the command line; the old parameter refuses the boot naming its successor.
- **Breaking** — `parameter.go`: the defaults name the kernel's directories as the framework spells them, `%kernel.project_directory%/generated_conf/cron/crontab` and `%kernel.logs_directory%/cron`; the resolved paths are unchanged. A `melody.cron.*` value of the application's own still naming `%kernel.project_dir%` or `%kernel.logs_dir%` refuses the boot as an undefined parameter.
- **Breaking** — `validation.go`: `ForbiddenCharacter.Char` is renamed `ForbiddenCharacter.Character`; nothing else changes.

### Removed

- **Breaking** — `parameter.go`, `module.go`: `ParameterRegistrar`, `RegisterDefaultParameters` and `ModuleConfig.WithDefaultParameters`; the module no longer implements `ParameterModule`; a parameter of one of the ten declared names that the application registers before `Boot` is refused naming the key that feeds it, and one a parameter hook registers is the duplicate of the aggregated boot report.
- **Breaking** — `validation.go`: the deprecated aliases `ForbiddenChar`, `CrontabForbiddenChars` and `ValidateNoForbiddenChars` are removed; `ForbiddenCharacter`, `CrontabForbiddenCharacters` and `ValidateNoForbiddenCharacters` remain.
- **Breaking** — `errors.go`: `ErrSharedRunnerCommandFlags`, deprecated and answered by nothing since the runner accepts a command answering the same flag instances.

### Fixed

- runner: a panic while a tick is gated by its dedup, the application's logger recording a skipped tick included, is the entry's failed row, `cron: the runner panicked gating a scheduled tick`, where it ended the process; under `TickDedupFailureRun` a logger panicking on the run-mode warning is swallowed and the tick still runs.
- runner: a sibling failure's cause chain renders each pointer link once, breadth-first, as the framework's chain does, where a chain reaching itself repeated its links up to the bound of eight.
- runner: a scheduled command's error whose `Unwrap` chain reaches itself no longer holds the dispatch worker that classifies it, nor the command that renders it.

### Documentation

- `README.md`: a Doors section with the v4 spellings, `LogDisabled`, `LogFileName`, `LogFileNameRaw`, `Instances`, `User` and `EntryUserIgnored`, the generator's `DirectoryMode` and `LogsDirectoryCheckDisabled`, `--prune` and `lock.NewExclusiveCommand`, and the reasons the `LogFileName` escape and the busybox day pair have no door.
- configuration: the GoDoc of `EntryConfig` states that a nil `Schedule`, like a field left out, runs the entry every minute, where it said the field was required.
