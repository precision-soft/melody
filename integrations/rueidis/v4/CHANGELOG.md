# Changelog

All notable changes to `precision-soft/melody/integrations/rueidis/v4` will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `lock.go`: `Locker` answers `lockcontract.SharedLeaseLocker`, true: its leases live in Redis, reached by every replica.
- `plaintext_address.go`: `Provider.Open` without `ClientConfig.TlsConfig` over an address that is not `localhost` or a loopback address writes the warning `rueidis.plaintextAddress` (`PlaintextAddressBootWarningName`) once per address of the provider in the runtime's journal, naming the address and never a credential, and honouring `MELODY_BOOT_WARNINGS_IGNORED`; `Module.RegisterBootWarnings` declares the code, the module now a `BootWarningModule`.
- `ClientMustFromRuntime(runtimeInstance)`, the raw client read through the runtime's scope under its context, for code that holds a runtime; a nil runtime is refused. `ClientMustFromResolver` stays the door of a provider.
- **Breaking** — `service_resolver.go`: `ClientMustFromContainer` and `TokenStoreMustFromContainer` are removed, their `…FromResolver` forms taking the container as they take a scope.
- This major includes every change of the third major up to v3.6.0; that history is in the third major's changelog.
- `client_config.go`: `ClientConfig.ClientSideCacheBytes`, the client-side cache's budget for each connection, 128 MiB when left out as the library's default, at least 1, unlimited refused, and refused naming both fields when the cache is not enabled.
- `cache/service_resolver.go`, `cache/module.go`: `BackendConfig.ScanCount` and `DeleteBatch` reach a registered backend, which registration always built with the defaults.
- `cache/backend.go`: `cache.ErrCallTimeout`, in the chain of the error of a round trip `BackendConfig.CallTimeout` cut and never of one the caller's own deadline or cancellation cut; it unwraps to `context.DeadlineExceeded` and is a timeout.

### Fixed

- `token_store.go`: `PurgeExpired` walks every node of the client on that node's own cursor, as the cache backend does, where a cluster client routed each step of the one `SCAN` to an arbitrary node and stale index members were left behind.
- rate limit: a store failure whose `Unwrap` chain reaches itself no longer holds the limiter that classifies it. The provider's open returns on such a failure too, from a TLS verifier for one.
- `README.md`, `module.go`: under `BootFailureWarns` a cli process builds the connection at its first use, and `BootFailureFailsBoot` connects in every process, a command included; the two documents were silent on it.
