# Changelog

All notable changes to `precision-soft/melody/integrations/rueidis/v4` will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- This major includes every change of the third major up to v3.6.0; that history is in the third major's changelog.
- `client_config.go`: `ClientConfig.ClientSideCacheBytes`, the client-side cache's budget for each connection, 128 MiB when left out as the library's default, at least 1, unlimited refused, and refused naming both fields when the cache is not enabled.
- `cache/service_resolver.go`, `cache/module.go`: `BackendConfig.ScanCount` and `DeleteBatch` reach a registered backend, which registration always built with the defaults.

### Changed

- `cache/backend.go`: **Behavioural change**: the context-less `Clear` and `ClearByPrefix` spend `BackendConfig.CommandTimeout` on each `SCAN` and `DEL` round trip, so the size of a namespace is not capped by it, as the third major's optional timeout capped it over the whole walk; the `Ctx` doors keep the caller's one context over the walk.
- `lock.go`: **Behavioural change**: `Locker` declares `lockcontract.LeaseLocker`, so `NewLeaderGate` refuses an unlimited ttl over it at construction.
- **Breaking** — `service_resolver.go`: `ClientMustFromResolver`, `ClientMustFromContainer`, `TokenStoreMustFromResolver`, `TokenStoreMustFromContainer` and `EpochRevocableTokenStoreMustFromResolver` take a `context.Context` first, the fourth major's resolver form; a provider passes `container.ContextOf(resolver)`.
- **Breaking** — `service_resolver.go` and `cache/service_resolver.go`: `ServiceRegistrar.RegisterService` takes the framework's `RegisterOptions` struct last, the fourth major's registration form, where it took variadic `RegisterOption` functions; the application satisfies it as before.
- **Breaking** — `cache/backend.go`, `cache/backend_service.go`: `Backend.Set`, `SetCtx`, `SetMultiple` and `SetMultipleCtx` and the `BackendService` twins take the ttl as a `bound.Duration`, the fourth major's cache contract form, read through `cache/contract.ResolveTtl`, so an absent ttl and one under a second are refused with the in-memory backend's answer and unlimited stores with no expiry, where the third major read 0 as no expiry, refused a negative ttl as `cache ttl is negative` and floored a sub-millisecond one to a millisecond.
- **Breaking** — `lock.go`: `Locker.CreateLock` and the lock's `Refresh` take the ttl as a `bound.Duration`, the fourth major's lock contract form, resolved through `lock/contract.ResolveLeaseTtl` at `Acquire` and `ResolveLeaseRefreshTtl` at `Refresh`: 30s when left out, at least 1ms, and an unlimited one refused naming `Locker.CreateLock.ttl` or `Lock.Refresh.ttl`, where the third major refused a non-positive ttl as `redis lock requires a positive ttl` and floored a sub-millisecond one to a millisecond.
- **Breaking** — `token_store.go`, `nonce_guard.go`: `RedisTokenStore.Put` takes the ttl as a `bound.Duration`, the fourth major's token store contract form, resolved through `security/contract.ResolveTokenTtl`, so an absent ttl and one under a second panic naming the ttl and unlimited stores with no expiry, where the third major's `Put` stored with no expiry and `PutWithTtl` refused a non-positive ttl as `redis token store ttl must be positive`; `NonceGuard.Remember` takes the ttl as a `bound.Duration` resolved through `security/contract.ResolveNonceTtl` and answers an absent, sub-millisecond or unlimited ttl as the error, where a non-positive one ran a read-only existence check.
- **Breaking** — `provider.go`, `provider_config.go`, `client_config.go`, `timeout_config.go`, `retry_config.go`: `NewProvider` takes one `ProviderConfig` of `Client`, `Timeout`, `Retry` and `Logger`, whose zero value is the default, resolved once, a limit outside its rule panicking naming the field before any client is built, where it took variadic options over three pointer configurations. `ClientConfig`'s zero value is now the default: `BootPingDisabled` and `ClientSideCacheEnabled` replace `PingOnStart` and `DisableCache`, so a partial literal keeps the boot ping and leaves the client-side cache off; `DialTimeout` and `ConnWriteTimeout` are bound limits, 5s when left out, at least 1ms, unlimited refused. `TimeoutConfig`'s `ConnectTimeout` and `CommandTimeout` are bound limits, 3s when left out, at least 1ms, unlimited refused, where a non-positive one took 3s. `RetryConfig` takes `MaxAttempts`, 3 when left out, at least 1, and `InitialDelay` and `MaxDelay`, 500ms and 5s, at least 1ms, as bound limits, unlimited refused, and a `BackoffMultiplier` below 1 or NaN is refused where it fell back to 2.
- **Breaking** — `provider.go`: an absent `RetryConfig` is the default retry, three attempts, where it made a single attempt; a single attempt is `MaxAttempts: bound.Of(1)`, and it journals its terminal failure as the last attempt of a retry does.
- **Breaking** — `connection.go`, `cache/backend.go`, `cache/backend_service.go`, `server_sent_event_backplane.go`: `Connection`, `cache.Backend`, `cache.BackendService` and `ServerSentEventBackplane` carry `CloseWithContext` in place of `Close`, the fourth major's one teardown door. The connection closes the client on a goroutine of its own and answers the deadline's error past it, the client's close going on in the background, a panicking client close answered as the close's error; the backplane clears itself from the hub under the deadline and waits for its listener under the same deadline, where `Close` cleared under the hub's own timeout and waited for the listener without a bound.
- `provider.go`: the dialer and the write deadline are always installed from the resolved values, so the library's own five-second dial and ten-second write defaults never govern a melody client, and a refusal reports the dial timeout as the one installed.
- **Breaking** — `lock.go`, `nonce_guard.go`, `token_store.go`: `NewLocker` takes a `LockerConfig` of `CallTimeout`, `NewNonceGuard` a `NonceGuardConfig` of `KeyPrefix` and `CallTimeout` and `NewTokenStore` a `TokenStoreConfig` of `Prefix`, `ScanCount`, `CallTimeout`, `Clock`, `MaximumClockSkew` and `RevocationEpochRetention`, resolved once and panicking naming the field: the call timeouts 1s when left out, at least 1ms, unlimited refused, where a non-positive one took 1s; `ScanCount` 256, at least 1, where a non-positive one took 256; `MaximumClockSkew` 0, at least 0, a negative one refused naming the field; `RevocationEpochRetention` 7 days, at least 1s, where a sub-second one was kept.
- `token_store.go`: `TokenStoreConfig.RevocationEpochRetention` admits `bound.UnlimitedDuration()`, which keeps a revocation boundary for ever, as the in-memory store does; a boundary an earlier retention gave an expiry is made persistent by the next revocation.
- **Breaking** — `service_resolver.go`, `module.go`: `RegisterLockerService` takes a `LockerConfig` and `RegisterTokenStoreService` a `TokenStoreConfig`, resolved at the call, so a value outside its rule or a nil client fails the registration where it failed the first resolve; the container's clock reaches a token store whose config names none. `ModuleConfig.Locker` and `ModuleConfig.TokenStore` replace `LockerOptions` and `TokenStoreOptions`.
- `rate_limit.go` and `server_sent_event_backplane.go`: the log records are built through `exception.LogContext(err, exception.LogContextConfig{...})`, the fourth major's form, which takes the extra context in a struct; nothing of this module's own surface changes.
- **Breaking** — `rate_limit.go`: `NewRateLimiter` takes the client and a `RateLimiterConfig` of `Limit`, `Window`, `KeyPrefix`, `FailureMode`, `OnError`, `Logger` and `CallTimeout`, resolved once, a value outside its rule panicking naming the field: `Limit` at least 1 and `Window` at least 1ms, both required, the in-process `LimiterConfig` form; `FailureMode` `FailureModeClosed` when left out; `CallTimeout` 250ms, at least 1ms, unlimited refused, where a non-positive one took 250ms.
- `rate_limit.go`: a window under a millisecond is refused where it was raised to 1ms, a failure mode other than the two constants is refused where it was read as closed, and an empty `KeyPrefix` is the default `melody:rate_limit:` where `WithRateLimiterKeyPrefix("")` kept the counters under no prefix.
- **Breaking** — `server_sent_event_backplane.go`, `reconnect_config.go`: `NewServerSentEventBackplane` takes the client, the hub and a `ServerSentEventBackplaneConfig` of `Channel`, `Logger`, `Reconnect` and `CallTimeout`, the amqp backplane's name, resolved before the backplane is installed on the hub, so a refused value panics naming the field and leaves the hub without a backplane; `CallTimeout` 1s, at least 1ms, unlimited refused, where a non-positive one took 1s; a typed-nil `Logger` records nothing, as a nil one. `ReconnectConfig` is a value whose `InitialBackoff` and `MaxBackoff` are bound limits, 1s and 30s, at least 1ms, unlimited refused, and whose `BackoffFactor` is 2 when left out.
- `reconnect_config.go`: an initial backoff above the cap is refused naming both fields where it was clamped onto the cap, and a backoff factor below 1 or NaN is refused where it fell back to 2.
- **Breaking** — `cache/backend.go`, `cache/backend_service.go`, `cache/service_resolver.go`, `cache/module.go`: `NewBackend` takes the context first, then the client and a `BackendConfig` of `Prefix`, `ScanCount`, `DeleteBatch`, `MaxKeyLength` and `CommandTimeout`; `NewBackendService` takes the client and a `BackendConfig`; `RegisterBackendService` takes a `BackendConfig`, resolved at the call, so a value outside its rule or a nil client fails the registration where a nil client failed the first resolve; `ModuleConfig.Backend` replaces `Prefix` and `BackendOptions`. `ScanCount` 1000, `DeleteBatch` 500 and `MaxKeyLength` 1024 when left out, at least 1, unlimited refused, refused naming the field where a non-positive value took the default.
- **Breaking** — `cache/backend.go`: `BackendConfig.CommandTimeout` bounds every operation dispatched without a caller context, each round trip of a `Clear` on its own, 1s when left out, at least 1ms, unlimited refused, where a backend built without `WithCommandTimeout` ran those operations unbounded.

### Removed

- **Breaking** — `provider.go`, `client_config.go`, `timeout_config.go`, `retry_config.go`: `ProviderOption`, `WithClientConfig`, `WithTimeoutConfig`, `WithRetryConfig` and `WithLogger`, folded into `ProviderConfig`; `DefaultClientConfig`, `DefaultTimeoutConfig`, `DefaultRetryConfig` and `NewRetryConfig`, since a zero configuration is the default; `ClientConfig.PingOnStart` and `DisableCache`, inverted.
- **Breaking** — `connection_parameters.go`: `NewConnectionParameters`, the deprecated `NewConnectionParams` and the deprecated `ConnectionParams` alias; the `ConnectionParameters` literal is the door.
- **Breaking** — `lock.go`, `nonce_guard.go`, `token_store.go`, `service_resolver.go`: `NewLockerWithOptions`, `LockerOption` and `WithLockerCallTimeout`; `NewNonceGuardWithPrefix`, `NewNonceGuardWithOptions`, `NonceGuardOption`, `WithNonceGuardKeyPrefix` and `WithNonceGuardCallTimeout`; `TokenStoreOption` and its six options; `RegisterLockerServiceWithOptions`; each folded into its config.
- **Breaking** — `token_store.go`: `RedisTokenStore.PutWithTtl`, folded into `Put`, and `WithTokenStoreContext`, since every door bounds its round trip by the call timeout and the context-less doors derive from the background context.
- **Breaking** — `rate_limit.go`, `server_sent_event_backplane.go`, `reconnect_config.go`: `RateLimiterOption` and the five `WithRateLimiter` options, folded into `RateLimiterConfig`; `ServerSentEventBackplaneOption` and the four `WithServerSentEventBackplane` options, folded into `ServerSentEventBackplaneConfig`; `DefaultReconnectConfig` and `NewReconnectConfig`, the `ReconnectConfig` literal being the door.
- **Breaking** — `cache/backend.go`, `cache/service_resolver.go`, `cache/module.go`: `BackendOption`, `WithMaxKeyLength` and `WithCommandTimeout`, folded into `BackendConfig`, and `RegisterBackendServiceWithOptions`, folded into `RegisterBackendService`.
